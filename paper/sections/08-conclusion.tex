\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
agentnet attributes network connections to AI-agent executions and uses the same identity for live accounting and an in-kernel egress policy. Against an independent destination-based reference, fusion turns faulty labels into reported ambiguity where the process structure is informative, the task-scoped binding removes the misattributions of thread-keyed markers for asynchronous tools in a shared MCP server, and policy and accounting stay correct with concurrent LLM agents. Injected binding faults stay confined to the faulty calls, and the in-kernel lineage check stops the bypasses that contradicted labels would otherwise cause. The cost is microseconds per connection, against tool calls of milliseconds and model steps of seconds. The artifact will be made publicly available upon publication.
